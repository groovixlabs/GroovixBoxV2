Step parameters R4

How hard each step hits, how long its notes last, and how many times it fires. The steps stay on top; velocity and gate each get two rows of 16 pads below them.

  • Tap a step to select it (green); tapping another moves the selection. Hold several steps to edit them together. Tapping here never switches a step on or off — that stays in Note mode.
  • Rows 5–6 — velocity: 16 levels, left to right along row 5 and then row 6, from 8 up to 127. The lane shows the value as a bar, and the steps above show their velocity as brightness, so the accents of a whole page show at a glance.
  • Rows 7–8 — gate: how long the step’s notes sound, as one ramp of 16 lengths from shortest to longest, a pad each. One tap is that length, like every other lane — nothing is hidden behind a second tap, and the bar shows how long the note is without your having to count.

    The resolution sits where the ear is: sixths inside the step, where the difference between a stab and a blip is obvious, and every other step at the top, where 13 and 14 sound the same.
  • If a long note is still sounding when the same pitch plays again, it stops first.
  • R5 + step resets its velocity, gate and ratchet; R6 + step + step copies all three to another step.
  • Hold SHIFT and rows 7–8 become the ratchets, the same rows the gate uses: pad n makes the step fire n + 1 times, spread evenly across its own length. Two is 32nd notes, four is 64ths and eight is a roll — the pads past eight stay dark. Each hit lasts its own gate or the space to the next one, whichever is shorter, so a long gate rolls instead of blurring, and a short one gives a stutter of stabs. The probability is rolled once, so a ratcheted step plays whole or not at all, and the whole thing moves with the step's micro-timing. R5 + step puts velocity, gate and the ratchet back.
  • Hold R4 and press B1–B8 for another page of steps. Tapping R4 on its own stays here — this is its mode. SHIFT + R4 opens Preset mode; R3 returns to Note mode.
  • Probability has its own mode, SHIFT + R5.
