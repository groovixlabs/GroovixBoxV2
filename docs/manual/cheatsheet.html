The eight buttons · R1–R8

R1Project

Opens Project mode. Hold it and press B1–B8 to jump to project page 1–8, from any mode.

SHIFT + R1 → Global settings

R2Pattern

Opens Pattern mode, and returns to it from Scenes.

SHIFT + R2 → Scenes, from any mode, Pattern included

R3Note

Opens Note mode, and returns from Scale. SHIFT + the top pad row picks its page of 32 steps.

SHIFT + R3 → Scale mode

R4Params

Opens Step parameters for the selected track. SHIFT + the top pad row picks its step page.

SHIFT + R4 → Preset mode, where holding R4 picks the preset page instead

R5Clear

Hold and tap a pad to clear that step, pattern, project, scene or song step. Orange while held. There is no undo.

SHIFT + R5 → Probability

R6Duplicate

Hold, tap the source pad (it turns white), then tap one or more destinations. You can change page in between.

SHIFT + R6 → nothing: the APC keeps it for its own pad mode

R7Record

Record on or off. While playing, note pads and a MIDI keyboard write into the selected track and the playhead runs red.

SHIFT + R7 → nothing, for the same reason as R6

R8Play

Play or stop. Playback always starts from step 1; stopping releases every sounding note.

SHIFT + R8 → the Song. Pressed in Song mode, R8 plays the arrangement.

Modes · what the pads do

Note

R3
  • 1–4 32 steps of the selected track
  • 5–8 the keyboard, rising from the root at bottom left
  • tap stepSwitches an empty step on. Tapping never switches one off — R5 + step does.
  • hold + keysHold a step and tap keys to give it that chord, up to 4 notes. Or hold keys and tap steps.
  • R3 + stepHold R3, tap a step: it becomes the pattern's last. Turns red, the steps after go dark.
  • SHIFT + keyTop-left key up an octave, bottom-left key down — the way the notes run. Both light white while SHIFT is held.
  • SHIFT + top rowPad 1–8 goes straight to that page of 32 steps, and the row shows which page you are on while SHIFT is held — green there, dim blue for a page with steps, grey for empty.

Piano roll

Scale row 8, pad 1
  • all 8 steps across, 8 notes of the track's scale up
  • SHIFT four pads at the bottom right become the arrows
  • tap padAdds that note to the step, or takes it out. Hold to hear it without changing anything.
  • SHIFT + arrowsLeft (8,6), down (8,7), right (8,8), up (7,7) — an inverted T. Up and down move the notes, left and right the steps, 4 at a time. Dark where there's nowhere to go.
  • R3 + padMakes its step the pattern's last. SHIFT does nothing to any pad but the four arrows.
  • noteOne of three ways to use the grid — it replaces chords, and pairs with Drums or Own scale.

Scale

SHIFT + R3
  • 1–2 the root note: black keys above white
  • 5–7 the 24 scales, Major first, in reading order
  • 8 what the keyboard is
  • row 8, 1PIANO ROLL — sits alone at the left end, because it changes the grid rather than the keys.
  • row 8, 5–6TRIAD and 7TH: one key then plays a whole chord, built from the scale.
  • row 8, 7–8DRUMS (two 4×4 blocks) and OWN SCALE (this track in another key).
  • noteRoot and scale belong to the project. Chords, Drums and the roll replace each other.

Step parameters

R4
  • 1–4 the steps; brightness is velocity
  • 5–6 velocity, 16 levels from 8 to 127
  • 7–8 gate, one ramp of 16 lengths
  • tap stepSelects it. Hold several to edit them together. Steps never switch on or off here.
  • gate ramp1–5 a sixth … five sixths · 6 one step · 7–11 two to six · 12–16 8, 10, 12, 14, 16 steps.
  • SHIFT + 7–8The same two rows become ratchets: pad n fires the step n+1 times, evenly.
  • R5 + stepPuts velocity, gate and ratchet back.

Probability

SHIFT + R5
  • 1–4 the steps; brightness is the chance
  • 5–6 6% … 94%, and 100% on the last pad
  • 7–8 micro-timing, in ticks of a 24th of a step
  • straightThe 9th pad of the lane — the first of row 8. Left is early, right is late.
  • the rungs±1 to ±4 is feel · ±6 and ±8 · ±10, ±12 is half a step, for flams.
  • tripletsA beat is 96 ticks, so every triplet position sits exactly 8 ticks off a step: use ±8.
  • R5 + stepBack to 100%. The grid itself never moves — only when the notes play.

Pattern

R2
  • cols the 8 tracks of the track page
  • rows the 8 patterns of the pattern page
  • green one per column: the pattern that track plays
  • amber pages 5–8: the built-in bank, read-only
  • tap padThat track plays that pattern at once, keeping the playhead. It selects the track too.
  • B1–B8Page the tracks — the columns — all eight pages. No SHIFT needed, and SHIFT changes nothing.
  • SHIFT + row 1The top pad row becomes the pattern pages — the rows — and shows which one you are on.
  • pages 5–8The built-in bank, lit amber: drums on track 1, bass 2, chords 3, arp 4, fills 5, repeating every 8 tracks. Read-only — R6 copies one down to pages 1–4 to edit.
  • SHIFT + row 8The bottom pad row becomes the mutes. A muted track's whole column dims.
  • R6Copies a pattern, also to another track or page.

Scenes

SHIFT + R2
  • 1–4 32 scenes: grey empty, blue held, green playing
  • 8 the 8 tracks of the track page — tap to mute
  • tap sceneLaunches it at the top of the next bar. It blinks until it lands; tap again to change your mind.
  • R7 + padCaptures what you are hearing into that pad. The scene pads turn red while R7 is held.
  • holdsWhich tracks are silent, and which pattern each track plays. Saved with the project.
  • R2Returns to Pattern mode. Mutes stay put as you flip between the two.

Song

SHIFT + R8
  • 1–4 the 32 scenes again — the palette to build from
  • 5–8 the 32 song steps, four bars each, in its scene's colour
  • hold + sceneHold a song step and tap a scene to put it there. The step's current scene lights while you hold.
  • R8Plays the song from the marker. Empty steps are skipped and it loops at the end.
  • tap stepGoes there at once. Tapping a scene takes over and the song stops advancing.
  • noteA scene has to hold something before it can go in the song.

Project

R1
  • all 64 slots of a page, 8 pages, 512 projects
  • green the open project — slots read page.slot, so 3.08
  • tap slotOpens that project, saving the open one first. An empty slot starts a new project there.
  • pagesSHIFT + B1–B8, or hold R1 + B1–B8 from any other mode to open straight onto a page.
  • R5 + slotClears it. The file is not deleted — it moves to trash/<date>_<time>/ in the data folder.
  • R6Copies a project, across pages too. Copying the open one includes unsaved changes.

Preset

SHIFT + R4
  • all 64 voices a page, 8 pages a window, 8 windows
  • green the one in use — a preset is a voice in the gear, so no pad is full or empty
  • tap padSends that voice: Bank Select CC 0, then CC 32, then the Program Change that latches them.
  • orderPick the track with B1–B8 first, then SHIFT + R4. A tap of R4 here goes on to Step parameters.
  • pagesHold SHIFT or R4 and press B1–B8 — 64 voices each.
  • SHIFT + row 8Picks the window: 512 voices each, 8 of them. Green is the window on screen, blue holds voices, grey is past the end of the list.
  • the listvoices.conf names a CSV per device — the maker's own list. Slot 1.01 is its first voice. No list means General MIDI, 128 voices.
  • noteR5 and R6 do nothing here. A patch lives in the synth, not in the sequencer.

Global settings

SHIFT + R1
  • 1 pick a setting: 1 tempo · 2 MIDI · 3 swing · 4 devices · 5 arp
  • 3–7 the picked setting's value
  • 8 pad 5 +1 · pad 6 −1 · pad 8 sends every CC again
  • fader 1Sets the picked value and sends no CC while it does. Nothing is picked until you tap the top row.
  • MIDIRows 3–4 channels 1–16 · row 5 ports P1–P8 · rows 6–7 instruments I1–I16. A track uses one or the other.
  • devicesRow 3 the gear · row 5 the ports that found something · row 7 INT / AUTO / EXT · row 8 pad 1 REFRESH.
  • arpRow 3 the mode · row 5 the rate, down to 1/64T · row 7 how many octaves.
  • B1–B8The one mode where they only move the selection, so you can set each track in turn.

The mixer, the tracks, the limits

Mixer panel

Eight strips to the right of the grid. Strip n is track n of the current track page. An Akai MIDI Mix is this panel in hardware and is picked up on its own.

  • K1–K3Knob rows: Cutoff (74), Resonance (71) and Pan (10, centred) by default.
  • faderVolume (CC 7), on the strip's track channel and port.
  • MUTEMutes the track at once. Mixer SHIFT + MUTE solos instead; several tracks can be soloed.
  • A2The page row — REC ARM on a MIDI Mix. It mirrors B1–B8, so in Pattern mode it is the track pages.
  • M1–M3Nothing yet.

Track colours

Eight colours, repeated on every track page: tracks 9–16 look like 1–8, and so on to 64. No track is red, so the markers that are — the last step, Record, the recording playhead — can never be mistaken for one.

  • 1 peach
  • 2 orange
  • 3 yellow
  • 4 green
  • 5 cyan
  • 6 blue
  • 7 purple
  • 8 pink
  • 64 tracks
  • 64 patterns each
  • 256 steps
  • 4 notes a step
  • 32 scenes
  • 512 projects
  • 512 presets
  • 20–300 BPM
A bar is 16 steps. A step is 24 ticks; a beat is 4 steps, 96 ticks. Clearing a project or preset moves the file to the trash folder — everything else is gone for good.
