Preset mode SHIFT + R4

Pick the patch the selected track’s gear plays. 8 pages of 64 pads reach the 512 patches that Bank Select and Program Change can address between them.

  • Select the track first with B1–B8 (which goes to Note mode), then SHIFT + R4. A tap of R4 here goes on to the step parameters, which is what the button means everywhere else.
  • Tap a pad to send that patch number to the track’s gear, on its channel and port: Bank Select — MSB (CC 0) = pad ÷ 128 and LSB (CC 32) = 0 — then Program Change = pad mod 128 (see Reference). The bank is a 14-bit pair that the receiver latches when the Program Change arrives, and it keeps the half it isn’t sent, so both go out together rather than leaving a stale LSB to decide the bank. It is sent again whenever the track’s channel, port or instrument changes, and for every track when a project opens, so your gear comes back on the right patches.
  • Opening Preset mode shows the page of the track’s current preset. Hold SHIFT (or R4) and press B1–B8 to change page.
  • Every pad looks the same but the one in use, which is green: a preset is a number, not something the sequencer keeps, so there is nothing for a pad to be full or empty of.
  • R5 and R6 do nothing here — there is nothing to clear or copy. In every other mode they act on something the sequencer holds; a patch lives in the synth.

A bank is 128 patches — two pages — and the split is invisible on the pads. The simulator’s status line spells out the two numbers being sent, as PRE 3.01 B1:0: page 3 pad 1 is bank 1, program 0. Gear that numbers its patches from 1 will show one more.
